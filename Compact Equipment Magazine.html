New Holland Construction Announces Five New Machines, Including a New Mini Track Loader

By: Pam Kleineke Published October 20, 2026
Listen to audio version of this article

New Holland Construction has introduced five new machines: the C324 mini track loader, F60C and F80C rough terrain forklifts and W50D and W60D compact wheel loaders. The releases are designed to give customers a range of options for moving bulk materials, loading trucks, handling pallets and working in environments where maneuverability, visibility and versatility are critical.

“Today’s operators need hauling and handling equipment that works harder and does more,” says Jake Sickels, product manager for New Holland Construction North America. “With greater lifting capacity, enhanced hydraulic performance and increased jobsite versatility, the New Holland Construction lineup expansion will further our presence in material-handling and on ever-evolving landscaping jobsites.”  

// ** advertisement **//

“Material handling looks different from job to job, which is why flexibility is so important,” says Dan Kakareka, product manager for New Holland Construction North America. “Whether an operator is loading mulch, moving pallets, handling turf and stone, loading trucks or working on a landscape project, these additions to our lineup give them a machine with customizable attachments built for the task.”

C324 Mini Track Loader

New Holland Construction’s new 43-hp C324 mini track loader offers 2,400 lbs of rated operating capacity and 4,800 lbs of lifting capacity. It will be available in both wide-track (51 in. wide) and narrow-track (47 in. wide) configurations. The company noted the C324’s larger frame builds on the proven design and engineering of its C314 and C314X (eMTL) mini track loaders, but provides additional rated operating and lifting capacity. This allows customers and operators to cover more square footage on larger commercial jobs while maintaining the ground visibility, maneuverability and transportability of a mini track loader.

From the press release:

Higher hydraulic performance allows the C324 to operate a wider range of high-performance attachments, including high-capacity stump grinders, trenchers, brooms, tillers and other rotary attachments. This machine features 13 gpm of standard flow and 21 gpm of high-flow capability to ensure it is an exact match for the range of CNH attachments. The higher hydraulic performance makes this machine well suited for commercial landscaping, arborist and forestry work, material movement and other applications where operators need one compact machine capable of performing multiple — and intensive — tasks.

New Holland highlighted that attachment adaptability is built into the C324, with connect-under-pressure hydraulic fittings that make attachment changes easier. Buyers can opt for a common-industry-interface (CII) attachment plate or a traditional skid steer attachment plate — allowing them to match the machine to their existing attachments or application needs.

The C324’s rear standing platform enables operators to maintain an all-around view of the machine, helping them work efficiently around landscaping, structures and other obstacles. New Holland said the C324’s compact size and transportability also mean it can be hauled by a half-ton pickup truck, depending on the trailer and transport configuration.

// ** advertisement **//

W50D and W60D Compact Wheel Loaders

New Holland Construction is also introducing its new W50D and W60D models — rounding out the D-Series compact wheel loader lineup. The company said these two models give customers more options to match machine size, performance and versatility to their operations.

The W50D and W60D are designed with compact frames and are ideal for landscaping, construction, material handling and agriculture. Both models boast a redesigned cab with a pilot-controlled seat and right-hand console that moves with the operator for improved control.

Other features include:

  • a standard rearview camera
  • an A-post-mounted monitor
  • return-to-dig
  • return-to-height
  • return-to-dump
  • ride control
  • float

A range of tire and attachment options are available for both models, including skid steer and horizontal-pin couplers, making them well suited for use cases such as truck loading, pallet handling, mulch loading, snow removal and general material moving.

More on the Machines

According to the press release, all five of these new machines come standard with seven years of complimentary telematics connectivity through myNewHollandConstruction. This platform gives owners and operators access to in-depth machine data, such as machine location, operating hours, maintenance schedules and machine health information. Notable features like geofencing, curfew monitoring and maintenance alerts are also included to help fleet managers make more informed decisions about their fleets and help keep jobs moving on schedule. 

Three of the new machines — the C324 mini track loader, F80C rough terrain forklift and W50D compact wheel loader — will make their public debuts at this week’s Equip Exposition in Louisville. Attendees can see them at New Holland Construction’s booths #7068 (indoor) and #7538D (outdoor).

Ready to add one of these machines to your fleet? Prospective buyers can now order the W50D and W60D compact wheel loaders. Ordering for the forklifts is also currently open with delivery for Q4 2026. The C324 mini track loader can be ordered beginning in spring 2027
