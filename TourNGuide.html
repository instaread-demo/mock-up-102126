Best Places to Visit in Delhi

Delhi has been an important part of the country and its history. Be it the numerous emperors or the political relevance, Delhi holds a special place for the country. The capital city of India offers a beautiful blend of history and modernity. On the one hand you would get to explore the impeccable monuments and architecture that was built by several rulers and on the other hand you will get to see the fancy dining, wonder parks, and lots of fun places to chill. No matter what you like, modernity or history, you will find Delhi to be a perfect place to explore the history of the country along with some fun places to have a great time with friends and family.

Well, if you are traveling to Delhi and wondering which places you must visit to make the most out of your trip then we have got you covered. Take a look at the following list of places to visit in Delhi and schedule your trip accordingly! 

1. Akshardham Temple

Akshardham temple is the perfect place to start your Delhi trip. The temple is dedicated to Bhagwan Swaminarayan and is quite one of the beautiful temples in the country to visit. The temple exhibits the ancient culture of our country and tells a tale in the most poetic way with portraits of heroes and warriors. 

The temple is nothing less than an architectural marvel with its marble carvings and beautiful sculptures. It is made in a step well style which makes it quite an unique experience. The light and fountain show is also an amazing experience to have here and it is organized every evening. 

2. Red Fort 

Red Fort is an iconic fort for this country. It was built in the 17th century by the Mughal emperor and is located in the old Delhi. The fort got its name from the red sandstone color. The octagonal shape of the fort is quite unique and looks beautiful from the top. Although made by a mughal emperor the architecture and paintings will give you hints of Persian, Hindu, and Timurid traditions too. You should also see the museum that features the mughal era artifacts including weapons, art, and belongings of emperors.

3. Hauz Khas

If you are looking to have some fun and chill with your friends then Hauz Khas must be the right place for you to explore. This place has got it all with its vibrant nightlife featuring clubs, cafes, and fine dining restaurants. It is a quite modern place but it still holds the charm of Delhi’s history and heritage with the remnants of mughal architecture. Overall, this place is worth your time and promises to be a good time for everyone! 

4. Worlds of Wonder

Worlds of Wonder is one of the best amusement parks of the country which you must check out if you like the thrilling rides and enjoy it with your friends or family. The place is filled with 20 rides, some might look scary at first but they are all quite fun to experience. There is a kid friendly area where your kids can enjoy and have a good time. The water rides are also amusing and will make you forget everything for a while. There are amazing food courts that you can check out in between the rides. 

5. Connaught Place

Commonly known as CP, Connaught Place is another must visit place in Delhi that you can’t afford to miss out on. The place offers some of the finest restaurants and street food corners as well. But it is mostly popular for hosting the most expensive showrooms featuring local and international brands. So if you love shopping, this might be your first stop in Delhi. 

6. Dilli Haat

Dilli Haat is a market corporation run by the Delhi Tourism and Transportation Development Corporation. It is an outdoor market place where you can find the finest artifacts made by some of the most talented artisans in the country. This place offers a wide range of artifacts including jewelry, decorative pieces, art, clothing, and so much more. You can also catch some musical and traditional performances here. 

7. Snow World

Last but not the least, Snow World is also a place that you must explore to get an unique experience. Located in the DLF Mall Noida, it is Asia’s largest indoor snow park. You can try out ice skating and sledding here. This place is no less than a snow wonderland and everything looks magical here. Make sure to try out some of the amusing rides because they are quite thrilling and fun. 

Conclusion

So these are some of the places in Delhi that you shouldn’t miss out on. We have covered some of the iconic places along with some hidden gems so make sure to check them all out and explore Delhi the right way. That is all for now and we hope you found the article helpful and got everything you have been looking for.
