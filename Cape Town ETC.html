    • New indoor play park opens at Somerset Mall in Somerset West
    Published by Hope Makhoba on October 5, 2026
     17  0
    Listen to audio version of this article
    Sponsored by
    0:00 / 2:13
    Instaread

    Families looking for a new way to keep children entertained can now add Freedom Adventure Park to their list of places to visit, with the indoor play park having opened at Somerset Mall in Somerset West.

    The new attraction caters to children between the ages of 0 and 13, offering a mix of play areas, sports activities and obstacle-style challenges under one roof.

    Plenty to keep kids moving

    Freedom Adventure Park has activities aimed at different age groups, including a dedicated Toddler Town and toddler trampolines for younger children.

    Older kids can make their way through the soft-play area, try indoor soccer or take on the Ninja Run obstacle course.

    There are also interactive activities such as Aero Strike and a Multiball sports wall, while children who enjoy putting can take on the nine-hole Putt Putt course.

    For something a little different, the park also offers UV Cosmic Glow sessions.

    The indoor setting could make the venue particularly useful for families looking for an activity when outdoor plans are affected by Cape Town’s unpredictable weather.

    How much does Freedom Adventure Park cost?

    Families can choose from several ticket options depending on how long they want to stay and which activities they want to try.

    The listed prices are:

    • 1-hour Toddler Pass: R100
    • 2-hour Toddler Pass: R150
    • 1-hour Play Pass: R130
    • 2-hour Play Pass: R200
    • Putt Putt: R150 per round
    • 1-hour Combo + Putt Putt: R240
    • All-Day Full Park Pass: R350

    Non-slip socks are required. Visitors who do not have their own can purchase a pair at the park for R50.

    For families in Somerset West and surrounding areas, the new venue offers a range of indoor activities in one location, from toddler play and soft play to sports, obstacle challenges and Putt Putt.

    Details:

    • Location: Somerset Mall, Somerset West, Western Cape
    • Age range: 0 to 13 years
    • Times: Monday to Saturday: 9:00 to 20:00 | Sunday: 09:00 to 18:00
    • Non-slip socks: Required; R50 at the park if needed
