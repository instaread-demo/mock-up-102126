Labyrinthine Puzzle Answers (Chapters 1-6)

Looking for Labyrinthine puzzle answers? This guide lists the important puzzle solutions from Chapters 1–6, including fixed answers and quick instructions for puzzles where the solution changes during your game.

Labyrinthine Puzzle Answers by Chapter

Chapter 1 Puzzle Answers

Cryptograph: SACRIFICE

Enter SACRIFICE in the first cryptograph to progress and get the key needed for the next part.

Lights Out puzzle: Turn on all the red tiles. The exact moves depend on the starting pattern, so there is no single button sequence for every game.

Chapter 2 Puzzle Answers

Greenhouse chemical puzzle:

Blue → Red → Green → Red → Yellow → Blue → Red

Use the chemicals in this order to complete the greenhouse puzzle.

Cages puzzle: Activate the pedestals in the correct sequence. The sequence can vary, so follow the clues in your game rather than using a fixed number order.

Five-token puzzle: Find all five tokens and place them in the central pedestal.

Chapter 3 Puzzle Answers

Chapter 3 mainly uses keys and items instead of fixed number codes.

Follow the key progression:

Hall Key → Pantry Key → Charles’s Room Key → Charles’s Drawer Key → Kitchen Key → Master Bedroom Key → Study Key

You also need to find and destroy 3 effigies. Afterward, use the ceremonial dagger at the crypt.

Chapter 4 Puzzle Answers

Memory puzzle: Match all 8 pairs of identical symbols.

Three-stone puzzle: Find the three sculptures and copy their symbols to the stone puzzle. The symbols determine the correct arrangement.

Five-key puzzle: Find all five keys, place them in the key station, and pull the lever. Move through the gate before it closes.

Chapter 5 Puzzle Answers

Chapter 5 does not use a simple password or code.

For the sluice gate puzzle, collect:

  • 4 gasoline cans
  • 6 sluice gate parts
  • Boat keys

Repair the sluice gates, drain the water, and continue to the boat.

For the sawmill puzzle, find the two lever sticks, operate the sawmill, cut the log, and use it to cross the gap.

Chapter 6 Puzzle Answers

Slide puzzle: Move the piece containing the key symbol to the locked position.

Circular skull puzzle: Arrange the plates until they form a skull.

Skull altar: Find and place all 5 skulls on the altar.

Which Labyrinthine Answers Are Fixed?

Some answers are fixed, such as:

  • SACRIFICE — Chapter 1
  • Blue → Red → Green → Red → Yellow → Blue → Red — Chapter 2 greenhouse

Other puzzles are randomized or depend on the clues in your current game. For these puzzles, copying an answer from another player’s game may not work.

If a puzzle has a changing solution, this guide gives you the fastest way to identify the correct answer instead of giving you a potentially incorrect fixed code.
